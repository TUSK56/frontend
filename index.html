Sheet A-100Scope of workRev 03Art Vision

We don’t decorate a space. We take the brand’s visual world and engineer it into something that can be built, shipped and installed.

Every project starts from a brand’s existing identity — not ours. We translate it into a build spec: materials, tolerances, lighting, structure. The store and the campaign inside it are read as one continuous system, built to hold up under daily retail conditions rather than to look right on install day only.

The work sits between design and fabrication. Our team moves from concept sketch to shop drawing to physical build without handing the project between disconnected vendors — which is where most retail rollouts lose fidelity to the brand.

Contract
One contract from drawing to install — no vendor relay
Production
In-house shop, in-house install crews
Coverage
Cairo, Alexandria and the North Coast

A real build sequence — each stage produces something the next stage depends on.

01

Brand intake

02

Spec engineering
